## Fit judgement

Better than the earlier GFtbox surface for Smith Fig.4 because it is cell-level, has explicit PIN/auxin states, and separates pin1/local auxin/ablation panels. It is still not an exact original-parameter reproduction because the Smith supplementary parameter table and original VV project source were not available locally or through accessible PNAS supplement URLs.
